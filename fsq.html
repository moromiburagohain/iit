Q1. What is the E&ICT Academy IIT Guwahati?

The Electronics and ICT Academy (E&ICT) is an initiative of the Ministry of Electronics & Information Technology (MeitY), Government of India, established at IIT Guwahati to bridge the gap between academic learning and industry requirements in the field of Electronics and ICT.

Q2. Who is eligible to enrol in this course?

Eligibility typically includes working professionals, research scholars, students and individuals interested in the course domain. Specific prerequisites (e.g., knowledge of a programming language) are mentioned on the course description page.

Q3. What format does the online course follow?

The course is delivered through live online interactive sessions, pre-recorded video lectures, hands-on lab sessions, and assignments. Recordings of live sessions are usually provided for a limited time.

Q4. What is the typical duration and time commitment required?

The course Is of 01 year with an weekly engagement of 06-08 hours. The expected time commitment usually includes 04-06 hours of live lectures/sessions and additional time for assignments and self-study.

Q5. Are the classes held on weekdays or weekends?

This depends on the specific course. Many courses for working professionals are structured for weekends only (e.g., Saturday and Sunday), while full-time courses may run on weekdays. Check the course schedule for exact details.

Q6. What is the course fee, and what does it include?

The fee is specified on the course page and typically includes access to: live sessions, video recordings, course materials (PDFs/presentations), assignments, lab access (if applicable), and the final examination/assessment. GST is usually applicable and mentioned separately.

Q7. How can I register for the course?

Registration is done online through the official E&ICT Academy IIT Guwahati website. You need to fill out the application form and complete the fee payment (usually via an online payment gateway like NEFT/RTGS or UPI).

Q8. Is there any provision for a refund if I withdraw?

Refund policies are strict and course-specific. Generally, a partial refund may be possible if you withdraw before the commencement date. No refund is typically given once the course has officially started or for non-attendance. Refer to the cancellation policy on the registration portal.

Q9. Is the course fee the same for students and professionals?

Often, E&ICT Academy courses offer a discounted fee for students (UG/PG/Ph.D.) upon submission of a valid student ID card/proof of enrolment.

Q10.What software or tools are needed for the course?

Requirements vary by course (e.g., Python, MATLAB, specific IDEs, or simulation tools). Students are generally required to install the necessary open-source or trial version software on their personal computers. Specific installation instructions are provided before the course begins.

Q11. What are the minimum hardware/internet requirements?

* PC/Laptop: Windows 10/Mac OS/Linux with at least 4 GB RAM (8 GB is preferred).* Internet: A stable broadband connection (recommended 5 Mbps or higher) is essential for uninterrupted live streaming.* Tools: A working microphone and webcam (optional, but good for interaction).

Q12.Will a lab environment or cloud access be provided?

For courses requiring high-end computing or specialized licensed software (e.g., Machine Learning, VLSI design), cloud lab access or a virtual machine environment is often provided for the duration of the course to complete the hands-on sessions.

Q13. How is the final assessment conducted?

Assessment typically includes: 1) Assignments/Quizzes (mid-course), 2) Project Work (for longer courses), and 3) A Final Online Examination (MCQs and/or practical questions).

Q14.What are the criteria to receive the certification?

To receive the certificate, participants usually must meet three criteria:* Maintain a minimum of 75% attendance in live sessions.* Complete all assigned projects/quizzes.* Score a minimum passing grade (e.g., 60%) in the final evaluation.

Q15. Who issues the certificate, and what does it state?

The certificate is jointly issued by the E&ICT Academy, IIT Guwahati, and the collaborating organization/industry partner. It mentions the course name, duration, and the grade/status achieved.

Q16. When will I receive the final certificate?

Certificates are typically dispatched (either in hard copy or as verified digital copies) 4-8 weeks after the successful completion of the course and final assessment.
