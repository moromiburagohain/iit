Other Activities

1. FDP Structure and Content

The programs are designed to align with the National Education Policy (NEP 2020) and focus on crucial, high-demand technical areas:

Program Type Duration / Credit Target Audience Emerging Topics Covered
Faculty Development Program (FDP) ~40 Hours (4 Credits) Faculty in HEIs AI/ML, Data Science, Cyber Security, Chip Design, 5G/6G Wireless, Drones (UAS/RPAS), AR/VR, Medical Electronics
Refresher Course ~80–90 Hours (8 Credits) Faculty in HEIs Deeper, more extensive training in core or emerging areas
Joint FDP / External Course Varies Professionals, Students, Faculty Collaboratively offered programs such as Full Stack Software Engineering with AI (with partners like AlmaBetter)

2. Strategic Role of Joint FDPs (Phase II)

The Joint FDPs and the Hub-and-Spoke model are essential to the Phase II strategy for achieving nationwide impact:

Key Area Description
Spoke Centre Handholding Hub Academies (IITG) mentor and establish Spoke Centres at Tier-2/Tier-3 institutions by running initial FDPs, enabling local faculty to independently conduct programs.
Wider Beneficiary Base Joint/External courses target students, industry professionals, and government officials (often chargeable) to ensure long-term sustainability as per Project Completion Report guidelines.
